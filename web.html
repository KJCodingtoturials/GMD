Got Questions?

Web development FAQ

How long does a website take to build?

Landing pages and portfolios usually take a few days to a week. Agency websites and stores take one to three weeks, and larger systems like a school management system are scoped individually after we understand your requirements.

Can the school system be customised for my institution?

Yes. Every school works differently, so we tailor modules, fee structures, result formats and user roles to match yours. Request a demo on WhatsApp and we will walk you through it.

Will my website work on mobile phones?

Absolutely. Every site we build is mobile-first and tested on real devices, because most of your visitors will be on their phones.

Do you provide hosting, domain and support after launch?

We can help you choose and set up a domain and reliable hosting, and we stay available after launch for updates, fixes and improvements.

Can you also run ads and grow my website traffic?

That is our specialty. Your site is built with Meta Pixel and analytics ready, and our Meta Ads and social media teams can start driving customers to it right away.
